# Evaluation

Given once, when the user says stop. A normal chat answer in the debate's language, no widget. Honest and specific. Quote the user's own words briefly where it helps. Around 300 to 500 words plus sources.

## Structure

**1. Verdict.** One of these, then two or three sentences why.
- Held. The core of the thesis survived every serious attack.
- Held with changes. It survived, but only in a narrower or more precise form.
- Wavered. Important attacks stayed open.
- Fell. The central claim was refuted or conceded.

**2. Your strongest moments.** Two or three moments where the user's argument was best, and why they worked.

**3. Where you were vulnerable.** The attacks that stayed open or were only partly answered, most serious first. For each, one line on what a good answer would have needed.

**4. What the Ally gave you.** Only if the Ally spoke. The arguments or facts worth keeping, so the user can use them without help next time.

**5. Evidence profile.** How the level moved through the debate, for example "Reasoning in rounds 1 to 3, figures from round 4." Say whether the thesis needs more evidence than the user brought, or whether it is mainly a question of values where reasoning was the right level.

**6. Stronger version of your thesis.** Rewrite the motion so it survives the attacks that landed. One or two sentences.

**7. Before you defend this for real.** Up to three things to research or prepare, as concrete as possible.

**8. Sources.** Every source any side cited, grouped by speaker, each as title and link. Leave out the section if no sources were used.
