# Open sources (tier A)

High-quality sources Claude can reach without a login. Search with `site:` filters where a domain is given. Fetch the page or PDF before using a figure.

## Reliability tiers

| Tier | Examples | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Official statistics, regulators, audited annual reports, filings, peer-reviewed papers | Cite directly |
| 2 | Investor presentations, central bank and international organisation research, established working paper series, top business press | Cite, cross-check numbers that carry the argument |
| 3 | Consulting firm reports, industry associations, trade press | Cite with the publisher named, treat forecasts as their view |
| 4 | Market research press releases, Statista, Wikipedia, blogs, SEO pages | Signpost only, find the original |

## Company sources

| Source | Covers | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Investor relations pages | Annual reports, investor presentations, earnings call transcripts, guidance | Search "[company] investor relations annual report [year]" |
| SEC EDGAR | 10-K, 20-F, 10-Q for US-listed firms | sec.gov/edgar |
| National registers | Filed accounts of private firms | Companies House (UK, free), Unternehmensregister (DE), CMVM (PT listed firms) |
| OpenCorporates | Basic company records worldwide | opencorporates.com |
| Company websites | Products, pricing pages, store counts, press releases | Official domain only |

Press releases from the company itself are tier 2 for facts about the company and tier 4 for claims about the market.

## Regulators and industry bodies

Competition authorities, sector regulators and industry associations publish market data that is often better than paid reports. Search "[industry] [country] regulator market report" or "[industry] association annual statistics". Examples for Portugal are ANACOM (telecom), ERSE (energy), Banco de Portugal (banking), Turismo de Portugal (tourism).

## Academic sources

| Source | Use |
|---|---|
| Scholarly connectors (Consensus, Scite, Elicit, SciSpace) | Use first if connected, they search and summarise papers |
| OpenAlex | Open index of papers with citation counts, api.openalex.org |
| Semantic Scholar | Paper search with citations and abstracts |
| Crossref | Verify DOIs and metadata |
| Unpaywall, CORE | Find legal free full texts |
| SSRN, NBER, IDEAS RePEc | Working papers in economics, finance, management |
| arXiv | Preprints in CS, economics, statistics |
| DOAJ | Open access journals |

Google Scholar blocks automated access. Do not fetch it. Give the user a Scholar search link instead when it would help.

Signals of a strong paper are a peer-reviewed journal, a high citation count for its age, and a known author or institution. Prefer the published version over the preprint when both exist.

## Institutional and think-tank research

ECB Economic Bulletin, IMF and OECD working papers, BIS, Bruegel, CEPR VoxEU, Brookings, PIIE, World Economic Forum reports. Tier 2.

McKinsey Global Institute, BCG, Bain, Deloitte Insights, PwC, Accenture research. Tier 3. Useful for frameworks and survey data, but often written to sell services. Name the firm when citing.

## News and business press

| Source | Note |
|---|---|
| Reuters, AP | Fast, factual, mostly free |
| Financial Times, The Economist, Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg | Strong but paywalled. Use free snippets only as a pointer, the user may have library access |
| BBC Business, The Guardian business | Free, good for context |
| Handelsblatt, FAZ, Les Echos, El País Economía, Expansión | Country business press |
| Jornal de Negócios, ECO, Expresso, Público, Observador, Jornal Económico | Portugal |
| Trade press (for example TechCrunch, The Verge, Retail Dive, Grocery Dive, Skift) | Sector news, tier 3 |

News is good for recent events and quotes, weak for market figures. Trace any number in an article back to its original source.
